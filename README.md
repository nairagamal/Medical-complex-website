# 🏥 Healthcare Center Website

A modern, responsive healthcare website designed to provide patients with an accessible and user-friendly digital experience. The website presents medical services, doctors, appointment booking options, career opportunities, and essential information about the healthcare center through a clean and professional interface.

The design features a blue-and-white medical theme, an Arabic interface, and right-to-left (RTL) layout support.

## ✨ Features

- **Modern UI/UX:** Clean, professional, and user-friendly interface tailored to the healthcare industry.
- **Arabic RTL Support:** Arabic interface with right-to-left layout.
- **Responsive Design:** Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- **Medical Services:** Dedicated section showcasing the healthcare center's services and specialties.
- **Doctors Section:** Displays doctors and their medical specialties.
- **Appointment Booking:** Provides an interface for patients to request appointments.
- **Careers & Job Opportunities:** Dedicated section for showcasing available job vacancies.
- **Job Details:** Organized presentation of job information and requirements, where available.
- **Patient Testimonials:** Displays patient feedback and experiences.
- **Why Choose Us:** Highlights the center's strengths and commitment to patient care.
- **Statistics Section:** Presents key healthcare center statistics.
- **Contact Section:** Provides essential contact information and communication options.
- **Professional Footer:** Organizes important links and website information.

## 🩺 Website Sections

1. **Home** — Introduction to the healthcare center.
2. **Medical Services** — Overview of available medical services and specialties.
3. **Our Doctors** — Information about doctors and their specialties.
4. **Why Choose Us** — Key benefits and features of the center.
5. **Statistics** — Highlights important center statistics.
6. **Book an Appointment** — Appointment request interface.
7. **Careers & Job Opportunities** — Career opportunities and job vacancy listings.
8. **Patient Testimonials** — Patient reviews and feedback.
9. **Contact Us** — Contact details and communication information.
10. **Footer** — Quick links and additional information.

## 🎨 Design & User Experience

- **Design Style:** Modern, minimal, and professional.
- **Color Palette:** Blue and white.
- **Language:** Arabic.
- **Layout:** Right-to-left (RTL).
- **User Experience:** Clear navigation, structured content, and accessible information.
- **Responsive Layout:** Adaptable interface for different screen sizes.
